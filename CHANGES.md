# Alterations from the Zenodo publication

The Zenodo source and mirrored `paper.pdf` are unchanged. With Brad Pierce’s authorization, the text editions replace the opening “Author and collaboration” and “Contribution statement” panels with a human author byline and the following section immediately before the original “Selected sources” bibliography:

## AI Assistance and Editorial Responsibility

This report was developed by Brad Pierce with substantial assistance from an AI model. He established the research objectives and editorial direction and retains responsibility for the claims, source attributions, and decisions about publication.

All substantive paragraphs, the complete abstract, eight original figures and their captions, 87 published references, and the concluding “Acknowledgment and scope” are preserved in their original order. The full removed wording, exact replacement, disclosure source, and representation changes are recorded in [changes.json](changes.json). Bibliographic front matter and relevance context are clearly separate repository additions.

The updated wording matches the current [reference article](https://bradpierce.ai/when-design-exploration-gets-cheap/) and its [GitHub HTML source](https://github.com/bradpierce-ai-eda/when-design-exploration-gets-cheap/blob/main/index.html). That repository’s paper.md contained older contribution wording at acquisition; it was not used as the modern-disclosure authority.
